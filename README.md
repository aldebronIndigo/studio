# 🚀 Aldebron Studio

Mobile-first SaaS for coaches, trainers & studios to manage operations, scheduling, and recurring revenue.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Most studio management tools are:

* Expensive (e.g. legacy platforms)
* Desktop-heavy and complex
* Built for marketplaces, not independent businesses

Coaches, trainers, and studio owners need something:

* Simple
* Mobile-first
* Built for daily operations, not listings

---

## ⚡ The Solution

**Aldebron Studio** is a mobile-first SaaS designed for modern service businesses.

It helps studios manage:

* Clients & memberships
* Classes & schedules
* Staff & instructors
* Attendance & performance
* Payments & revenue tracking

All from a single, easy-to-use system.

---

### 📅 Scheduling & Classes

## 🧠 Architecture

Built for scalability and speed:

* **Frontend:** FlutterFlow (mobile-first)
* **Backend:** Firebase (Firestore + Auth)
* **Subscriptions:** RevenueCat + Razorpay
* **Real-time Data:** Firestore streams
